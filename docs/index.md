## What's new {#features .cards}

Every change is optional. See all the [keys and options](changes.html) or the [full list](man.html#changes).

### [A modern look](man.html#appearance)

Rounded pills, a hover marker and a cleaner top line.

### [Choice prompts](man.html#choice-prompts)

Options show as pills, with the key to press underlined.

### [Floating windows](man.html#floating-windows)

Help, stats and confirmations open in rounded popups.

### [Git status](man.html#git-status)

Status symbols in the listing and the branch beside the path.

### [Readable timestamps](man.html#appearance)

Compact eza or US style, cycled with <kbd>y</kbd>.

### [Quieter detail view](man.html#display)

Hide directory sizes and permissions, or toggle them with <kbd>I</kbd> and <kbd>M</kbd>.

### [Updated preview plugin](https://github.com/amazebb/nnn/blob/amazebb/plugins/preview-tui)

Native splits in Ghostty and Rex, chafa or viu images, RAW previews.

### [Everyday conveniences](changes.html#keys)

Quick Look with <kbd>;</kbd><kbd>;</kbd>, copy paths, set sort keys and more.
